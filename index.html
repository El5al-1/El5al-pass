Privacy Notice

EL5AL PASS runs fully offline. All accounts are stored only in this device’s LocalStorage — nothing is uploaded, synced or tracked. LocalStorage is not encrypted: anyone with access to this device (or its browser data) can read the stored values. Your Master PIN is saved as a one-way hash, which deters casual access but is not real encryption. Do not store highly sensitive credentials on shared or rooted devices, and keep an exported backup in a safe place.
